Questions

Financing FAQs

Will applying for financing affect my credit?

Many lenders offer a soft-credit prequalification that doesn't affect your score. Accepting a loan usually involves a full credit check. Ask our team which applies before you apply.

Can I combine insurance and a payment plan?

Yes. Insurance pays its share first, and you can finance the remaining balance.

Do you charge for a consultation?

We don't charge for giving advice. Call us to discuss any aspect of your dental health and your options for paying for it.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our in-office savings plan covers your preventive care for one yearly fee and lowers the cost of most other treatment.
