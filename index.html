Welcome to

Primary PPC

Primary PPC is a premier dental clinic committed to providing high-quality dental care to patients of all ages. Our goal is to help every patient reach and keep a healthy, confident smile.

We use state-of-the-art technology, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, and our friendly, knowledgeable staff will walk you through your options and their cost before any treatment starts.

Our services

Complete care under one roof

Dental Implants

Permanent replacements for missing teeth: a titanium post and a custom crown that looks and bites like a natural tooth.

Learn more

Dentures

Full and partial dentures, relines and repairs, plus implant-supported dentures that stay secure while you eat and speak.

Learn more

Bridges

Close the gap left by a missing tooth with a fixed bridge anchored to neighboring teeth or implants. No removable parts.

Learn more

Technology

Digital X-rays with less radiation, intraoral cameras, and 3D CBCT scans for precise, guided implant planning.
